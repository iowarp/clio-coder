Multi-Process Capacity Leasing Protocol

Every worker dispatched across local and remote nodes must acquire a durable lease before spawning. Leases serialize through a single cross-process transaction lock (dispatch-admission.json.lock).

Heartbeat Protocol & Dead-Process Reaping

1. Heartbeat Interval (1,000 ms)

Active workers emit heartbeats every 1,000 ms over their control channel. The orchestrator advances heartbeatAt and extends expiresAt by 30 seconds.

2. Process Birth Tokens

The lease records ownerPid alongside a unique processBirthToken. If the OS reuses the PID after an unexpected crash, the mismatch triggers immediate lease reclamation.

3. Lazy Reaping on Admission

Every admission attempt inspects the process table and purges dead PID leases inside the cross-process lock before computing free slot capacity.

Cluster Drain & Maintenance Operations

Drain Mechanics (`clio-coder fleet drain`)

  • Active running jobs continue to completion without interruption.
  • New dispatch requests are refused with an explicit drain notice.
  • Auto-expires after 1 hour (DEFAULT_CAPACITY_DRAIN_TTL_MS) to prevent accidental deadlocks.

Resume Operations (`clio-coder fleet resume`)

Clears the drain state immediately and restores normal admission scheduling across all nodes.
