# Clio Coder report bundle consent, version 1

## debug

The maintainers of Clio Coder may read this bundle to diagnose and fix this report and closely related defects. It is
deleted 90 days after the issue closes, and 180 days after it was received whatever the issue's state. You can ask for
it to be deleted sooner with `clio-coder report forget <id>`.

## dataset

After review, the bundle's mechanics may also be included in public datasets that help people debug and train coding
agents. Anonymous ids are re-keyed and times are shifted before a release, and values seen in fewer than five bundles
are replaced. A published dataset cannot be recalled: `report forget` keeps the bundle out of every later release, but
a release already published stays published.

## transcript

The bundle includes the last turns of this conversation: your prompts and the model's replies, and the names of the
tools it called, after secrets and this machine's names and paths are masked. Your prompts and code may still say
things the masking cannot recognize. Tool arguments and output are left out unless you also tick them. With `dataset`,
this excerpt may be published too.
